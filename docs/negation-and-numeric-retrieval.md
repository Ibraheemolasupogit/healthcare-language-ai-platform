# Negation and Numeric Retrieval

Negation support is a lightweight deterministic feature layer. It detects bounded local markers such as `no`, `not`, `without`, `denies`, `negative for`, and `absence of`, then applies compatibility scoring between query and candidate polarity.

Numeric support identifies integers, decimals, percentages, and controlled units such as `mm`, `cm`, `kg`, `mg`, `mL`, `bpm`, and `percent`. It distinguishes exact numeric and unit matches from mismatches.

These rules do not perform clinical inference, medication interpretation, diagnosis, or treatment advice.
