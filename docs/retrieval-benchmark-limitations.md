# Retrieval Benchmark Limitations

The corpus is small, synthetic, and template-driven. Some direct queries overlap
with corpus vocabulary, while paraphrased queries are manually authored local
variants. Relevance judgments are manually authored from deterministic synthetic
lineage and have no clinician relevance assessment.

The benchmark is useful for retrieval engineering validation, but it does not
demonstrate real clinical-search performance, production latency, external
validity, or hosted embedding quality.
## Milestone 7 Limitations

The holdout is independently templated but still synthetic and locally authored. Vocabulary overlap remains and is disclosed. Relevance judgments are not clinician-reviewed. Retrieval configurations are tuned using development and validation evidence, while holdout metrics are excluded from selection. Synthetic metrics do not demonstrate clinical-search performance.
